Example

Three steps, one of them yours.

  1. 1

    You comment

    Under any public post on X (Instagram soon): @CommenterPad $TICKER Name. Up to 10 letters for the ticker, 32 for the name.

  2. 2

    It launches

    We see the comment within seconds and launch the coin on pump.fun, named from your comment, with the post's picture. It trades against X Coin.

  3. 3

    Fees burn it

    Every trade pays a creator fee, locked to the coin's own buyback wallet. The fees buy the coin back on its curve, and everything bought is burned.

The pairs

Every coin trades against its platform's coin, so every buy of a comment coin is a buy of the platform coin first. Prices are live from DexScreener.

Newest first. Each one started as a comment.

Fair launch rules

Every launch is paid for, so a comment launches a coin only when it passes these.

  • One coin per account per day. Per platform.
  • One coin per post. The first qualifying comment under a post wins.
  • No repeat tickers. A ticker can launch once a day.
  • Real accounts. On X, at least 30 days old with 10 followers.
  • Reserved names. $X, $INSTAGRAM, $TIKTOK and $SOL cannot be launched, and blocked words are refused.

CommenterPad launches pump.fun coins from public comments. A coin is named by whoever commented; its picture comes from the post it was left under. Nothing here is investment advice, and a coin's price can go to zero.
